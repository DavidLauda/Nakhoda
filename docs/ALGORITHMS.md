# Algorithm and quality contract

`environment-index-v0.2` is a design hypothesis. The pipeline reads real, overlapping OSTIA foundation SST and ocean-colour chlorophyll-a, rejects missing/land values, pairs time slices no more than 72 hours apart, and aggregates chlorophyll into a 0.05° common grid no finer than the SST source. A central SST gradient uses geographic meters. Valid-cell percentile ranks produce front strength; positive log chlorophyll percentile `p` produces `C=max(0,1-|p-0.60|/0.40)`. The index is `round(100*(0.60*front_rank+0.40*C))` with at least 20 valid cells and nonconstant spatial variation. Every finite cell in the pilot grid is published, up to an explicit 300-cell limit; the PWA displays only the newest acquisition pair and hides scores older than seven days. Unverified zoning makes each one **environmental exploration only**. It does not estimate catch probability, species suitability or safe passage.

`optical-change-v0.1` searches CDSE Sentinel-2 L2A for a recent scene within 30 days and a same-tile baseline 30–120 days earlier. It requests a shared EPSG:32748 grid at ~10 m for B02/B03/B04/B08, SCL and dataMask. SCL 0/1/2/3/7/8/9/10/11, invalid data and nonfinite reflectance are masked; shallow reefs can be misclassified, so water-only SCL is not required. The minimum overlap is 70%. A broad visible brightness shift over 0.08 suppresses candidate polygons. Stable-reference pixels are the lowest 40% of visible absolute differences; each visible band is standardized by reference median and `1.4826*MAD` with numerical floor `1e-4`. Median absolute standardized signal above 3 forms candidates after 8-connected components of at least nine pixels. A poor pair still publishes dated before/after and descriptive difference imagery with explicit quality flags, but no confident candidate region. Fixed display stretch is separate from numeric reflectance. There is no habitat mask, tide/depth/water-column correction or validation study; outputs are **coastal optical change in reef study areas**, never coral-damage claims.

`crw-heat-v0.1` extracts the nearest genuine NOAA CRW v3.1 0.05° cell for each study-square center, checking CF units, fill values, dates and the official alert code. SST and HotSpot remain °C, DHW retains °C-weeks, and the 7-day alert period is recorded separately. This context cannot confirm bleaching of a particular reef. The pipeline preserves source filenames/IDs, provider URLs, fetch/processing/publication times, algorithm and AOI versions, hashes, coverage and quality flags. A source failure never replaces a previously published record with zeros or a fabricated observation.
